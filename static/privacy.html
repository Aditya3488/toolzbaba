{{SITE_NAME}} ({{SITE_URL}}) offers free online tools for images, PDFs, video and files. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data. We keep it minimal.

Your files stay on your device

Every tool (images, PDFs, Word and Excel, video and audio, the AI tools, text tools and more) works entirely inside your browser. Your files are not uploaded: they are processed on your own device and we never see them. The only thing your browser downloads from us is the software that does the work, such as the PDF engine, the video engine and the AI models, so the first use of some tools takes a moment longer.

Image links (hosting)

The one exception is the "Image to CDN Link" tool, whose whole purpose is to put an image online. Images you upload there are stored with Cloudflare, our hosting provider, so the links keep working. Anyone who has a link can view the image, so do not upload anything private. An image is kept for 90 days, until you delete it (using the delete button in the tool, which works from the browser you uploaded from), or until it is removed under our Terms. To have an image removed, see Report content.

Cookies and local storage

  • We use Google Tag Manager to measure how the site is used (for example, which pages and tools are visited). The tags it loads, such as Google Analytics, may set cookies and send Google information like the page you visited, your browser and an approximate location. They never receive your files. You can block them with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker, and the tools keep working. See how Google uses this information.
  • Your light/dark theme choice and, for the image link tool, the list of images you uploaded and their delete keys are stored in your browser's local storage, on your device only.

Logs and security

Like any website, our hosting provider Cloudflare may record technical information such as your IP address, the time and the page requested. This is used to keep the site secure and prevent abuse, and is kept only for a limited period.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can stop using the site at any time, clear your browser's local storage to remove your saved image list, and ask us to delete a hosted image by contacting us.
